# EduPlay Design Guidance

## Design Context

### Users
EduPlay serves Romanian children ages 3–12 and the teachers, parents, and
administrators who support them. Children need approachable learning activities
with clear choices and encouraging feedback. Teachers need to create and assign
activities and understand progress quickly. Parents need practical visibility
into their child's learning. Adult tools should stay calm and easy to scan.

### Brand Personality
Playful, encouraging, trustworthy. Use warm, brief Romanian copy with correct
diacritics. Help children feel capable and adults feel confident. Vulpița is the
existing platform mascot; use her purposefully rather than as decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
EduPlay will find and develop its own design identity. Do not use another
product's appearance, page structure, or imported reference bundle as the target.
The current purple palette, Lexend typography, quiet surfaces, and shared
components describe the implementation baseline; they do not settle the future
visual direction. Explore changes through concrete EduPlay needs and coherent
design choices. No new reference product or replacement aesthetic is prescribed.

Read `edu-play-core/DESIGN.md` for current web implementation contracts and
`phaser-projects/.impeccable.md` for canvas-specific accessibility guidance.
Older imported prototypes and dated audits are historical evidence, not design
authority. Legacy identifiers do not establish visual requirements.

### Design Principles
1. **Build EduPlay's own identity.** Choose visual and interaction patterns for
   this product and its users; do not measure quality by resemblance to another app.
2. **Make children feel capable.** Keep choices simple, feedback encouraging,
   and primary touch targets at least 64px in the shell and 88px in game canvases.
3. **Give adults clear control.** Prioritize useful hierarchy, readable content,
   and efficient classroom and family workflows.
4. **Keep accessibility and privacy foundational.** Preserve WCAG AA contrast,
   visible focus, keyboard access, reduced motion, visual equivalents for audio,
   and child-facing experiences without third-party tracking.
5. **Evolve coherently.** Use shared tokens and Mantine-backed DOM primitives as
   the working baseline; update the system deliberately as the original design
   direction develops. Keep artwork and game canvases native to their renderer.
