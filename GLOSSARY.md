# EduPlay

EduPlay connects learners, teachers, and families through educational Activities.

## Language

**Activity**:
A learner-facing educational experience that can be played or answered.
_Avoid_: Game when referring to Activities generally.

**Activity Version**:
An immutable published edition of an Activity. An Assignment pins the edition the learner is expected to play.

**Activity draft**:
An editable Activity and its current resources, prepared before it is marked ready for assignment.

**Assignment**:
A teacher's request for a class or selected learners to complete a particular Activity Version, optionally by a due date.

**Free-response submission**:
A learner's text, drawing, or voice response to an assigned Activity Version, which a teacher can review or return for revision.

**Activity package**:
A reviewable bundle of Activity metadata, versioned content, localized text, and integrity-pinned assets prepared for publication.

**Activity streak**:
A consecutive run of calendar days in Europe/Bucharest on which a learner engages with Activities. The streak remains current when its latest active day is today or yesterday.

**Play session**:
One run of an Activity, from readiness until completion, exit, or restart. A restart begins a new play session.

**Active play time**:
Time spent playing within a play session, excluding loading and pauses. The total freezes when the Activity is completed.

**Learning Path**:
An ordered sequence of steps assigned to a learner or class. A learner progresses through the sequence, and a teacher may adjust or override that progress.

**Learning Path step**:
One educational experience in a Learning Path's sequence. Completing the current step unlocks the next step.

**Learning Path definition**:
The authored description and ordered steps that define a Learning Path.

**Activity progress**:
A learner's latest saved state for an Activity, including completion, score, and accumulated play time.

**Portfolio artifact**:
A learner's drawing, audio recording, or photograph kept for review and possible sharing with their family.
